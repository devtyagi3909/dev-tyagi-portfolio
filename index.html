About me

Silicon meets
Software Thinking

I'm a B.Tech ECE student at VIT Chennai (Aug 2023 – May 2027) with a relentless passion for hardware that thinks. I design and verify the RTL that sits at the bleeding edge — out-of-order RISC-V processors, neural network inference accelerators, and AXI4 DMA engines that push silicon to its physical limits.

My work spans the full RTL-to-silicon flow: SystemVerilog/UVM verification with constrained-random stimulus and SVA property checks, through synthesis, P&R, and timing closure on Xilinx UltraScale+. I've achieved 250 MHz post-synthesis closure and >95% functional coverage on a full superscalar OOO RISC-V processor.

Beyond the bench, I lead the Robotics Club at VIT Chennai — 40+ engineers, workshops, and intercollegiate competitions — because great hardware is always a team sport.
